New skills now have to prove themselves before they are added. When a change creates or edits a skill, the Skill Orchestrator sits at the High Table (a fast-lane change is raised to graduated so the seat is always called), and the development stage runs `paqad-ai skill` through nine gated stages: justify against three no-skill runs, intake, evidence with an overlap check, draft with a trace for every instruction, a deterministic lint (SK-001 to SK-019) plus contract tests in the project's own test framework, evals against a baseline on the project's fast and reasoning models, a trigger test, and release with tracked evidence. The end-of-change check now says "Needs your attention" when a new or edited skill has no passing or overridden record; a removed skill gets no check. You are only asked business questions (overlap, a risky script, a gap in the intake, an improvement, a retirement, an override), and a skill that cannot pass a gate is recorded as paqad's defect instead. On Claude Code a new PostToolUse hook logs when a skill fires, for `paqad-ai skill catalog`. Edits under `runtime/base/skills/**` in the paqad repository are gated the same way. Set `skill_orchestrator_enabled=false` to turn all of it off.
