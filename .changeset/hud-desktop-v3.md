The HUD now has its own design in the Claude Code Desktop app. The band is a card with who is working and what it costs, one line that says what is happening, and a strip of the stages, the High Table and the delivery steps that apply to the change. A live state breathes and shows a running clock. The close button asks inline. The paqad Ledger on Desktop has equal tabs for Trust, High Table, Delivery, Record and Cost, and `Answer in chat` puts the open questions in the prompt box. The HUD now draws even when another plugin owns `/paqad`: its command is then `/paqad-hud`, and every hint names it. If paqad cannot read the session's record, the band says so. `/paqad hide` hides the band and `/paqad delivery` is an alias of `/paqad ships`. On a feature change, each prompt reminds Claude of the lane's stage list, and a task list that leaves the stages out is turned back once per turn. The idle terminal band now reads `Getting ready`.
