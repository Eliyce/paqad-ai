# Goal-first playback template

The playback is how step 3 shows the user the shaped idea. It always starts with the goal, never
with a restatement of the request. Keep it compact and in business words (see `voice.md`). Fill
only the parts that apply; leave out an empty one rather than padding it.

## Shape

**Goal.** One sentence: who gets what value. This is the first line.

**What exists today.** A plain summary from the today block, including what the project documents
could not confirm. Name the unknowns rather than guessing past them. Say an unknown area as not
documented; never say or imply it is not built, and keep the label on every line from the code, for example
"the code suggests members can earn points".

**Options.** Two or three realistic ways forward. They become the closing choice, asked through
the host's choice prompt: each option a few words plus its trade-off in business terms only (time,
cost, risk, effect on users), the recommended one marked and first. No options table in the reply
unless the host has no choice prompt.

**Smaller first step.** Where the idea is large or uncertain, a smaller first release or an
experiment that tests the riskiest assumption, with the result that would justify going further.
Leave this out when the idea is already small and clear.

**Recommendation.** The option you would choose and one short clause of why. If the right outcome
is not tickets (already exists, experiment, process change, defer or stop), recommend that here.

**Order at the end.** The Sources line comes first, then the source line for the evidence level,
then the question ("Does that shape work, or another option?") as the very last thing, through the
choice prompt. Never put the question above the sources.

## Example shape (structure only, not real content)

- Goal: a short sentence naming the user and the value.
- Today: one or two lines on what the product does now and what is unknown.
- Options: the choice prompt options, recommended first, one clause of why.
- Smaller first step: one line, with its success condition.
- Recommendation: one line.
- Last: Sources line, then the question in the choice prompt.

Keep the whole playback short. Offer more detail in one line only if the user may want it.
