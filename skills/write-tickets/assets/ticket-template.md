# Ticket template

Use these sections for every ticket, in this order. Each ticket sits under exactly one epic. Keep
it in plain business words and follow the writing rules in `references/writing-rules.md`.

## Title

A verb plus the outcome. At most 80 characters.

## Epic

Exactly one parent epic.

## Who it's for

The role or persona.

## What they need and why

Plain sentences. "As a... I want... so that..." is optional.

## What exists today

Only the part relevant to this ticket, including the unknowns.

## Done when

2 to 8 checkable statements a tester could verify without asking the author.

## Not included

Required.

## Depends on

Other tickets in this set or existing tracker issues. These become tracker links.

## Open questions

Optional.

## Sources

As for the epic, with the item reference, for example `DR-20261005-7K2Q / E1-T2`.

Assignee is left empty unless the author names a person, who is then looked up through the tracker
connector.
