## What

The spec review detectors report 41 major defects on a well-formed spec: 40 `dangling_reference` defects say AC-1 to AC-24 (and the FR ids) are "not defined" although each is defined on its own `- AC-n:` bullet in the Acceptance criteria section, and one `contradiction` defect pairs "always" and "never" across two unrelated edge-case bullets because both end with "absorbed by". The frozen spec only keeps the counts, so a real defect would be hidden among the false ones.

## Expected

The dangling-reference detector treats a `- AC-n:` / `- FR-n:` bullet as a definition, and the contradiction detector compares statements about the same subject, not the shared word "absorbed". Re-running the review on this change's spec.md gives zero defects.
