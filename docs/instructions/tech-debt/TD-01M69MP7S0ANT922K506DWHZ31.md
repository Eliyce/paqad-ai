## What

runtime/hooks/skill-activation.mjs prunes activations.jsonl with a read-then-write once the file passes 1 MB. Two concurrent PostToolUse hooks (parallel subagents) can interleave an append between the read and the write, dropping that activation line. Low impact (one lost activation, only at the prune boundary), fail-open is unaffected. A rename-based prune or a lock would close it.
