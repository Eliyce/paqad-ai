# Shop platform requirements

Invented fixture content for testing only. Status: baseline for technical design.

## Loyalty points

- The system must let members earn points on every completed order.
- The system must let members redeem points at checkout.
- The system must let members gift points to another member.
- The system should show each member their points balance on a page of its own.

## Member accounts

- The system must let members create an account, sign in and edit their profile.
