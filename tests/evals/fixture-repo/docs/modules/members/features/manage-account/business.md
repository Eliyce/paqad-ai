# Manage an account

Invented fixture content for testing only. The project calls the people who sign in and shop
"accounts". A user may say "members" or "users" for the same thing; the project term is account.

## Roles

A shopper owns one account. A support agent can view an account and reset its sign-in. A security
job locks an account after repeated failed sign-ins.

## Business rules

- An account is identified by a verified email address and can hold several saved addresses.
- An account is locked for a cooling-off period after five failed sign-ins in a row.
- A support agent can reset sign-in for an account but can never read its stored password.
