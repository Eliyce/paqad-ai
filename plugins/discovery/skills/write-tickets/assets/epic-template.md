# Epic template

Use these sections for every epic, in this order. Each epic is one outcome. Keep it in plain
business words and follow the writing rules in `references/writing-rules.md`.

## Title

The outcome, not the activity. At most 80 characters.

## Goal

One sentence: who gets what value.

## Why it matters

The business reason, in the author's own words.

## What exists today

A plain summary from the today block, including what the project documents could not confirm.
Name the unknowns; do not fill them with a guess.

## In scope / Not in scope

Bulleted. "Not in scope" is required, even when it reads "Nothing excluded yet".

## How we'll know it worked

The author's success measures. Without a number, write "To be measured after release". Never
invent one.

## Decisions made

From the decisions block, each with who decided and why.

## Open questions

Each with a suggested owner.

## Sources

A plain section at the bottom: the document paths, the run ID and the item reference, for example
`DR-20261005-7K2Q / E1`.
